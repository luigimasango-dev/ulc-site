What we do

Bid & tender compliance

SBD pack assembly, returnable-schedule control, CSD and tax-status verification, mandatory-document checklists, and submission logistics. We track the binding deadline — usually the compulsory briefing, not the closing date.

JV & consortium facilitation

Where a tender needs a grading, registration or track record you do not yet hold, we source a compliant partner and structure the joint venture — then run the compliance workstream for the consortium.

Regulatory & partner due diligence

POPIA readiness assessments, B-BBEE scorecard gap analysis, and verification of a counterparty's registration, standing and public footprint before you sign a joint venture or subcontract.

Grant applications

Eligibility screening and proposal compilation against South African funding sources — SEDFA, NYDA, the dtic incentive schemes and the SETAs — including the mandatory-grant WSP/ATR cycle.

How we work

Engagements are scoped per tender or per retainer. Compliance work is quoted and invoiced on delivery, so you know the cost before the bid goes in. Where we broker a joint venture, a success fee applies on award — agreed in writing up front.

We do not submit on your behalf without sign-off, and we do not claim capability we cannot evidence. If a tender's functionality gate is beyond what your business can demonstrate, we will say so before you spend money on it.
