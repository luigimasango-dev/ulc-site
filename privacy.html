Privacy Policy

Ubuntu Lab Consultants (Pty) Ltd · Reg 2026/027426/07 · Effective 11 September 2026

Ubuntu Lab Consultants (Pty) Ltd ("ULC", "we") is a South African consultancy based in Katlehong, Gauteng. This policy explains what personal information we process, why, and the rights you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and collects no information from visitors. GitHub may log standard server requests (IP address, user agent, time) under its own privacy statement.

2. Email you send us

If you write to ubuntulabs.ai@gmail.com we hold your name, email address and the content of your message in order to reply and, where an engagement follows, to deliver the service. We keep correspondence for as long as the enquiry or engagement is live and for a further period as required by South African tax and company law, after which it is deleted.

3. Internal tools that use Google account data

ULC operates internal software ("ULC Gmail Draft" and related tools) that connects to Google services through Google's OAuth system. These tools are used only on ULC's own Google accounts by ULC staff. They are not offered to the public and do not access, store or transmit any third party's Google account data.

  • Gmail: the gmail.compose scope is used to create and manage email drafts in ULC's own mailbox. Drafts are reviewed and sent by a person; the tool cannot send email on its own.
  • Google Calendar: used to create and read events on ULC's own calendars.

Google user data obtained through these tools is used solely to provide the internal functionality described above. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, used to train machine-learning models, or transferred to any other party. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Client work

In the course of compliance, due-diligence and advisory work we may process personal information supplied by a client or drawn from public records (CIPC, National Treasury CSD and similar). We process it only for the purpose the client engaged us for, keep it no longer than that purpose requires, and apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards.

5. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction or deletion, and object to processing. Write to ubuntulabs.ai@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa), inforegulator.org.za.

6. Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date.
